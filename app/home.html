Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Everything you need to know about Phinmaed Learning before you get started.

Phinmaed Learning (Learning Management System) is the official digital platform of PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College. It allows students to access course materials, take quizzes, submit requirements, communicate with instructors, and track their grades — all in one place.
Phinmaed Learning is available to all officially enrolled students, faculty instructors, program deans, and system administrators of PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College. Only users with a valid school account can sign in.
Click the Register Here button and fill in your school ID, full name, COC email, and a password. Your account will be tied to your official enrollment record. Once registered, an administrator will verify and activate your account.
Yes — partially. Once you have visited your subjects while online, lessons and materials are cached and available to read offline. Messaging is queued and automatically sent when you reconnect. However, taking quizzes and joining new classes require an active internet connection.
Yes. Phinmaed Learning is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers. You can also install it as an app on your home screen using the "Add to Home Screen" option in your mobile browser — no download from an app store needed.
Phinmaed Learning supports all three PHINMA COC campuses: Carmen Campus, Puerto Campus, and Iligan Campus. Each campus has its own separate data environment managed by its dean, while sharing the same platform infrastructure.
On the Sign In page, click Forgot Password and enter your registered school email. You will receive a one-time password reset link. If you continue to have trouble, contact your campus administrator.
