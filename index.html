What is COC LMS?

A Learning Management
System built for PHINMA COC

LMS stands for Learning Management System — a digital platform where all academic activity happens in one place. COC LMS is the official platform of PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College, designed to make teaching and learning more structured, efficient, and accessible.

Whether you are a student submitting outputs, an instructor managing grades, or an admin overseeing enrollment — COC LMS is your single workspace for everything academic.

AI-Powered

AI Learning Assistant

An intelligent study companion built into the platform — ask questions, get guidance, and receive tailored support anytime.

Online Lessons

Structured modules with video content, materials, and real-time progress tracking.

Quizzes & Assessments

Auto-graded quizzes with timers and anti-cheat measures for fair evaluations.

Grade Management

Live gradebook for instructors with real-time visibility for every enrolled student.

Messaging System

Direct messages and announcements between students and instructors.

Class Enrollment

Seamless online enrollment with section management, offerings, and enrollment codes.

Terms of Use

By creating an account on COC LMS, you agree to use the platform solely for academic purposes as a student, instructor, or staff of PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. Unauthorized access, sharing of accounts, academic dishonesty, or misuse of the platform is strictly prohibited and may result in account suspension.

Privacy Policy

PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College collects your personal information (name, email, student ID, mobile number, and academic details) solely for the purpose of managing your academic account on COC LMS. Your data will not be shared with third parties without your consent, except as required by law. You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting info.coc@phinmaed.com.
