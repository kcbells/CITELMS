About Phinmaed Learning

A Smarter Way to
Learn and Teach

Phinmaed Learning is the official digital learning hub of PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College — connecting students, instructors, and administrators across all three campuses in one seamless platform.

Centralized Learning HubAccess lessons, materials, and quizzes anytime, anywhere.
Real-time Grade TrackingMonitor academic performance with live gradebooks.
AI Learning AssistantAI-powered help for students on coursework and subjects.
Multi-Campus SupportCarmen, Iligan, and Puerto — one unified system.
Take It With You

Learning That Goes With You

Phinmaed Learning brings lessons, quizzes, grades, enrollment, and messaging together in one connected system — so students, instructors, and admins can teach, learn, and track progress from anywhere, on any device.

Terms of Use

By creating an account on Phinmaed Learning, you agree to use the platform solely for academic purposes as a student, instructor, or staff of PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. Unauthorized access, sharing of accounts, academic dishonesty, or misuse of the platform is strictly prohibited and may result in account suspension.

Privacy Policy

PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College collects your personal information (name, email, student ID, and academic details) solely for managing your academic account on Phinmaed Learning. Your data will not be shared with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
